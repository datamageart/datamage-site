Custom projection activation across an assigned section of the 550ft Art Wall spanning the left interior wall of the main hall. Content built for the space, the crowd density, and three days of festival heat.

Project brief

What was built

DataMage occupied an assigned section of the 550ft Art Wall running the left interior wall of the main hall at Travis County Expo Center. Interior positioning protected projectors from weather across all three show days — critical on a weekend that came in with weather risk.

Custom motion content was mapped to the wall geometry and built around the energy of Illfest's crowd — high ambient-light optimized, loop-structured, designed to hold up across 8+ hour run times without visual fatigue. The activation ran full hours each day from load-in through last call.
